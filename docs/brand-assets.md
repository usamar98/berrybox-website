# Hero stock examples

The hero identifies Apple, Microsoft and NVIDIA as illustrative stock reward companies, not partners, sponsors or confirmed reward providers. The reward program is labeled as planned and links to the full on-page explanation.

The small monochrome marks are served locally from `public/brands`:

- Apple: SVG path from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/apple.svg).
- NVIDIA: SVG path from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/nvidia.svg).
- Microsoft: four-square symbol drawn as SVG geometry; reference: [Microsoft brand assets](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/legal/intellectualproperty/trademarks).

Simple Icons distributes its artwork under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md); this does not grant trademark rights.
